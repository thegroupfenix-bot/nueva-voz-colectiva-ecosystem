# Content Strategy

## Nueva Voz Colectiva Ecosystem 2026-2036

## 1. Executive Summary

Nueva Voz Colectiva Ecosystem requires a long-term editorial strategy to build institutional authority, public trust, civic legitimacy, and national and international credibility between 2026 and 2036.

The content strategy must position Nueva Voz Colectiva as a citizen ecosystem, leadership platform, territorial development organization, learning center, cooperation platform, and producer of applied knowledge. It must not be perceived as a political party, electoral campaign, assistance-based foundation, personal blog, or partisan opinion outlet.

The editorial system must connect education, evidence, transparency, cooperation, impact, and knowledge. It must support current institutional content and future platforms such as Campus Nueva Voz, Observatory, Strategic Thinking Center, Community, Certifications, Territorial Laboratories, and Cooperation and Sustainability mechanisms.

## 2. Structural Decisions Adopted

The following decisions are adopted as editorial foundations:

1. Donations will not be used as the institutional axis.
2. Cooperation and Sustainability will be used as the superior strategic concept for funding, partnerships, memberships, sponsorships, campus income, events, services, grants, and donations.
3. The Impact Ecosystem will be incorporated as a distinct editorial and institutional dimension.
4. Library, Observatory, and Strategic Thinking Center will remain separate:
   - Library organizes resources, publications, guides, methodologies, and educational materials.
   - Observatory manages indicators, monitoring, open data, reports, and program tracking.
   - Strategic Thinking Center produces applied research, studies, foresight, social innovation, and strategic analysis.
5. Multilingual architecture will be reserved for Spanish and English.
6. Spanish will be the primary editorial language in the first phase.
7. English will be introduced progressively for international cooperation, institutional summaries, impact reports, partner materials, and selected knowledge products.

## 26. Prohibited Content

Nueva Voz Colectiva must not publish content that weakens civic independence, public trust, or institutional positioning.

Prohibited content includes:

- Electoral endorsements.
- Candidate promotion.
- Party propaganda.
- Campaign messages.
- Vote requests.
- Partisan attacks.
- Ideological manifestos.
- Polarizing opinion columns.
- Personal political commentary.
- Content centered on a single leader as savior.
- Charity-centered content that presents communities as passive beneficiaries.
- Unverified accusations.
- Unverified statistics.
- Manipulated or decontextualized data.
- Content that exposes personal data without consent.
- Community stories without informed permission.
- Fundraising messages disconnected from transparency.
- Donor promises without approved projects or reporting capacity.
- Content that presents Nueva Voz Colectiva as the representative of all citizens.
- Generic motivational posts without institutional value.
- Clickbait headlines.
- Sensationalist crisis narratives.
- Content copied from external sources without attribution.
- AI-generated content published without review and institutional accountability.

## 28. Editorial Risks

### 28.1 Political Perception Risk

Risk: content may be interpreted as campaign or party communication.

Mitigation:

- Use civic, educational, and institutional language.

### 28.2 Assistance-Based Perception Risk

Risk: content may frame communities as passive recipients.

Mitigation:

- Emphasize capacity, autonomy, leadership, and co-creation.

### 28.3 Blog Drift Risk

Risk: the platform may become a collection of unstructured posts.

Mitigation:

- Publish through programs, projects, library, reports, and resources.

### 28.4 Authority Dilution Risk

Risk: publishing too much low-value content may weaken credibility.

Mitigation:

- Prioritize durable, useful, reviewed content.

### 28.5 Cooperation Credibility Risk

Risk: international audiences may find weak evidence or unclear governance.

Mitigation:

- Connect cooperation content to reports, transparency, and impact.

### 28.6 Data And Evidence Risk

Risk: publishing unverified indicators or exaggerated impact may damage trust.

Mitigation:

- Require evidence review and source documentation.

### 28.7 Multilingual Quality Risk

Risk: poor translations may affect international credibility.

Mitigation:

- Translate selectively and review strategically important English content.

## 29. Strategic Recommendations

1. Build authority through fewer, stronger, better-governed publications.
2. Treat Cooperation and Sustainability as the resource mobilization umbrella.
3. Keep donations as one mechanism, not the institutional message.
4. Develop the Impact Ecosystem as a bridge between programs, projects, observatory, transparency, and cooperation.
5. Maintain separation between Library, Observatory, and Strategic Thinking Center.
6. Avoid blog-style publishing as the main editorial model.
7. Create annual reports from the beginning, even if simple.
8. Build English content progressively for cooperation and international credibility.
9. Establish editorial review before publishing research, data, cooperation, and impact claims.
10. Use content to build institutional memory, not only visibility.
