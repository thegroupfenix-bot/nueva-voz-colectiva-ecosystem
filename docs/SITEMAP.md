# Sitemap

## Nueva Voz Colectiva Ecosystem

## 1. Executive Summary

This sitemap defines the public structure and navigation map for Nueva Voz Colectiva Ecosystem. It does not define interface design, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or visual layout. It defines the institutional structure, recommended URLs, SEO-friendly paths, navigation hierarchy, future routes, and public information organization required for a civic, educational, territorial, and international cooperation institution.

The sitemap is designed so Nueva Voz Colectiva appears as an international civic and territorial development institution, not as a blog, small foundation, political party, or electoral campaign.

The first public structure includes ten Level 1 sections: Inicio, Quienes Somos, Programas, Proyectos, Biblioteca, Eventos, Participa, Cooperacion Internacional, Transparencia, and Contacto. Future routes add Campus Nueva Voz, Observatorio, Comunidad, Certificaciones, Centro de Pensamiento, Laboratorios Territoriales, Centro de Recursos, Convocatorias, Donaciones, and Voluntariado.

## 2. Sitemap Principles

The sitemap must follow these principles:

- Institutional clarity.
- Public trust.
- SEO-friendly structure.
- Long-term scalability.
- Civic and non-electoral positioning.
- Clear separation between institutional, educational, territorial, cooperation, transparency, and participation routes.
- Stable Level 1 navigation.
- Future expansion without breaking the initial structure.
- Simple URLs in Spanish, with future multilingual readiness.
- Avoidance of blog-style or campaign-style architecture.

## 11. SEO-Friendly URL Rules

Recommended rules:

- Use lowercase URLs.
- Use hyphens between words.
- Avoid accents in URLs.
- Avoid generic slugs such as `/page1/` or `/post/`.
- Avoid dates in evergreen institutional content unless needed for events or reports.
- Use Spanish URLs for the first phase.
- Prepare future multilingual routes with language prefixes if needed.

Examples:

```text
/programas/liderazgo-ciudadano/
/proyectos/territoriales/agenda-local-de-juventud/
```

## 15. Risks

### 15.1 Blog Perception Risk

Risk: content may be organized as posts instead of institutional entities.

Mitigation:

- Use programs, projects, library, reports, and resources instead of generic blog structures.

### 15.2 Small Foundation Perception Risk

Risk: a shallow sitemap may make Nueva Voz Colectiva appear informal or small.

Mitigation:

- Use institutional, transparency, cooperation, and knowledge structures from the beginning.

### 15.3 Political Campaign Risk

Risk: participation routes may be interpreted as electoral mobilization.

Mitigation:

- Use civic education, volunteering, community, and learning language.

### 15.4 Overexpansion Risk

Risk: launching all future routes too early may exceed operational capacity.

Mitigation:

- Keep future routes planned but phased.

### 15.5 SEO Fragmentation Risk

Risk: inconsistent URL patterns may weaken discoverability.

Mitigation:

- Use stable, descriptive, and hierarchical URLs.

### 15.6 Transparency Weakness Risk

Risk: transparency may become symbolic if routes lack real reports or policies.

Mitigation:

- Publish routes progressively with real documentation and reporting.

### 15.7 Cooperation Credibility Risk

Risk: cooperation routes may look like fundraising without capacity.

Mitigation:

- Connect cooperation to projects, reports, partner criteria, and transparency.

## 16. Recommendations

1. Launch only Level 1 and essential Level 2 routes in the first phase.
2. Reserve future routes until the corresponding operational capacity exists.
3. Keep transparency in primary navigation.
4. Keep cooperation international as a primary institutional area.
5. Avoid a generic blog route as a central content structure.
6. Use Biblioteca, Centro de Pensamiento, and Observatorio for knowledge, research, and data.
7. Use Participa, Voluntariado, Comunidad, and Convocatorias for civic engagement.
8. Use Donaciones under sustainability, connected to transparency.
9. Maintain stable strategic URLs across the decade.
10. Prepare multilingual URL patterns before international expansion.
