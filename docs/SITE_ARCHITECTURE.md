# Site Architecture

## Nueva Voz Colectiva 2.0

## 1. Executive Summary

Nueva Voz Colectiva 2.0 must not be designed as a traditional website, personal blog, small NGO page, campaign site, or political communication channel. It must be designed as a scalable institutional ecosystem for leadership, education, citizen participation, territorial development, knowledge production, transparency, sustainability, and international cooperation.

The platform should begin with a clear public institutional architecture and progressively evolve into a ten-year digital ecosystem with educational, data, community, cooperation, and territorial capabilities.

The first version must establish public trust, institutional clarity, navigation discipline, and content foundations. Future phases should add Campus Nueva Voz, Observatory, Community, Certifications, Strategic Thinking Center, Territorial Laboratories, Advanced Digital Library, Resource Center, Calls Platform, and Volunteer Platform.

## 2. Architectural Principles

The architecture must follow these principles:

- Institution before interface.
- Ecosystem before isolated pages.
- Trust before promotion.
- Education before mobilization.
- Knowledge before opinion.
- Transparency before fundraising.
- Territorial relevance before generic messaging.
- Cooperation readiness before international promises.
- Modularity before complexity.
- Scalability before visual novelty.
- Civic identity before political visibility.

## 4. Navigation Levels

The navigation must be designed to support growth without overwhelming users.

### 4.1 Level 1: Primary Navigation

Primary navigation should include stable institutional areas:

- Inicio.
- Quiénes Somos.
- Programas.
- Proyectos.
- Biblioteca.
- Eventos.
- Participa.
- Cooperación Internacional.
- Transparencia.
- Contacto.

These sections should remain clear, visible, and stable during the first phase.

### 4.2 Level 2: Section Navigation

Each primary section should contain internal subsections.

Examples:

- Quiénes Somos: purpose, mission, vision, principles, team, governance.
- Programas: leadership, civic education, territorial development, participation, cooperation.
- Proyectos: territorial projects, educational projects, cooperation projects, research projects.
- Biblioteca: guides, publications, methodologies, reports, resources.
- Transparencia: governance, reports, partners, policies, results.

### 4.3 Level 3: Content Detail

Content detail pages should support specific institutional assets:

- Program pages.
- Project pages.
- Publication pages.
- Event pages.
- Report pages.
- Partner pages.
- Resource pages.
- News or institutional updates only if editorial governance exists.

### 4.4 Level 4: Future Platform Workspaces

Future advanced sections may require workspace-style navigation:

- Course dashboards.
- Certification paths.
- Observatory dashboards.
- Volunteer profiles.
- Community spaces.
- Calls and application workflows.

These should not be introduced until governance, content operations, data protection, and technical support are ready.

## 8. Relationships Between Sections

The architecture must connect sections intentionally.

### 8.1 Programs And Projects

Programs define recurring institutional lines. Projects demonstrate implementation.

Relationship:

- Each project should belong to a program or strategic line.
- Each program should show related projects and results.

### 8.2 Projects And Transparency

Projects generate evidence for transparency.

Relationship:

- Project pages should connect to reports, results, partners, and accountability summaries.

### 8.3 Library And Programs

The library supports learning and program quality.

Relationship:

- Programs should link to guides, methodologies, and publications.
- Library resources should indicate related programs.

### 8.4 Events And Community

Events are entry points into participation and community.

Relationship:

- Events should connect to programs, participation pathways, and resources.

### 8.5 International Cooperation And Projects

Cooperation should be grounded in actual programs and projects.

Relationship:

- Cooperation pages should show priorities, project portfolio, partner criteria, and reporting.

### 8.6 Observatorio And Transparency

The observatory provides data and monitoring that strengthen transparency.

Relationship:

- Observatory outputs should connect to reports, project tracking, and impact indicators.

### 8.7 Campus And Certifications

Campus provides learning; certifications validate learning.

Relationship:

- Certification pages should derive from learning paths and assessment models.

### 8.8 Centro de Pensamiento And Biblioteca

The Strategic Thinking Center produces studies and applied knowledge; the library organizes and preserves them.

Relationship:

- Studies, reports, and papers should be discoverable through the library and cited in programs, projects, and cooperation materials.

## 16. Architectural Risks

### 16.1 Political Perception Risk

Risk: the platform may appear as a political campaign or party structure.

Mitigation:

- Use institutional language.
- Avoid candidate-centered content.
- Keep participation civic and educational.
- Make transparency and governance visible.

### 16.2 Small NGO Perception Risk

Risk: the platform may look like a small informal organization without long-term capacity.

Mitigation:

- Build clear architecture.
- Document governance.
- Present programs and projects structurally.
- Show reports and accountability.

### 16.3 Overcomplexity Risk

Risk: adding too many future modules too early may overwhelm the team and users.

Mitigation:

- Phase advanced capabilities.
- Build only when governance and content exist.
- Keep the first navigation stable.

### 16.4 Content Fragmentation Risk

Risk: programs, projects, library, events, and cooperation may become disconnected.

Mitigation:

- Use shared taxonomies.
- Link related content.
- Define ownership by section.

### 16.5 Cooperation Credibility Risk

Risk: international cooperation pages may promise more than the organization can deliver.

Mitigation:

- Use phased language.
- Show evidence, governance, and reporting.
- Publish clear partner criteria.

### 16.6 Digital Maintenance Risk

Risk: the ecosystem may become difficult to maintain as it grows.

Mitigation:

- Use modular architecture.
- Define governance.
- Review content regularly.
- Avoid unnecessary platform sprawl.

### 16.7 Data And Privacy Risk

Risk: future observatory, community, campus, and volunteer tools may collect sensitive information.

Mitigation:

- Define data governance before launch.
- Collect only necessary information.
- Establish privacy and access controls.

## 18. Main Architectural Decisions

1. Nueva Voz Colectiva 2.0 is an institutional ecosystem, not a traditional website.
2. The first version must focus on trust, clarity, programs, projects, library, participation, cooperation, and transparency.
3. Future capabilities must be phased and governed before launch.
4. Knowledge, observatory, campus, community, cooperation, and sustainability must be connected but architecturally distinct.
5. The platform must avoid campaign, party, blog, and small NGO patterns.
6. Navigation must remain stable while deeper modules grow progressively.
7. Transparency must be a core public section, not a hidden compliance page.
8. Cooperation must connect to evidence, projects, reports, and partner criteria.
9. Participation must be civic and educational, not electoral.
10. The architecture must support Colombia, Latin America, and international cooperation over a ten-year horizon.
